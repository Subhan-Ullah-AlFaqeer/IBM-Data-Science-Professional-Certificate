## 📝 Overview
In this module, I investigated the operational impact of Big Data across modern society, ranging from corporate enterprises to advanced sports analytics. I developed a comprehensive understanding of the technological infrastructure—specifically cloud computing and distributed systems—required to manage massive datasets. 

Furthermore, I explored the core pillars of modern artificial intelligence, evaluating the distinct mechanics of data mining, classical machine learning, deep learning, and generative AI frameworks.

---

## 🎯 Key Milestones & Learning Objectives

### 🐘 Big Data & Cloud Infrastructure
*   **The V-Attributes of Big Data:** Defined and analyzed the core characteristics of Big Data: *Velocity, Volume, Veracity,* and *Value*.
*   **Distributed Computing:** Evaluated how **Hadoop** and parallel processing architectures act as catalysts for modern corporate digital transformation.
*   **Cloud Architecture:** Mastered the five essential characteristics of cloud computing environments tailored for data science workflows.

### 🤖 Analytics, Machine Learning & AI
*   **Data Mining Lifecycle:** Summarized the critical phases of data mining preparation, including goal setting, data selection, preprocessing, transformation, and storage.
*   **The AI Hierarchy:** Clarified the architectural boundaries and differences between Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Neural Networks (**Deep Learning**).
*   **Predictive Modeling:** Analyzed **Regression** methodologies and their mathematical applications in predicting market behavior and performing trend analysis.
*   **Generative AI Foundations:** Explored the fundamental concepts of generative models and their current industry applications.
