# 💼  — Module 1: Building a Foundation & Job Search Strategy

Welcome to Module 1 of **Data Science Career Resources & Job Search Strategy**! Setting up a strategic foundation before entering the job market significantly increases your search velocity and landing rate. This module focuses on understanding modern data scientist roles and career trajectories while crafting core professional artifacts: targeted resumes, adaptable cover letters, portfolio showcases, and high-impact elevator pitches.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
* **Role & Trajectory Mapping:** Deconstructing data science specialization paths, core daily responsibilities, and industry demand patterns.
* **Portfolio & Work Sample Curation:** Packaging raw machine learning projects, SQL queries, and capstone analytics into employer-ready portfolio assets.
* **Professional Artifact Construction:** Drafting tailored resumes, customizable cover letters, and concise elevator pitches aligned with modern technical recruiters' standards.
