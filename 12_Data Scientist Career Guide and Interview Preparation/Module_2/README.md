# 🎯 — Module 2: Job Seeking & Interview Preparation

Welcome to Module 2 of **Data Science Career Resources & Job Search Strategy**! This module focuses on the tactical execution of your job search: identifying high-potential opportunities through company research, expanding your reach via professional networking, and mastering the interview lifecycle through structured rehearsal and behavioral preparation.

---

## 📝 Core Technical Objectives
* **Strategic Opportunity Assessment:** Evaluating job descriptions for cultural fit, technical alignment, and growth potential to focus application efforts.
* **Professional Networking:** Building and leveraging industry connections via informational interviews to uncover "hidden" job market roles.
* **Interview Performance Optimization:** Developing structured responses to technical and behavioral questions through rehearsal techniques.

## 🎯 Technical Skills Architecture

### 🔎 Job Seeking & Networking
* **Market Research:** Analyzing organizational health, data team composition, and product roadmaps to validate "good fit" opportunities.
* **Informational Interviewing:** Developing professional inquiry templates to request time with industry peers for career guidance and referrals.
* **Application Lifecycle:** Navigating various submission types (ATS vs. direct email) and tracking application stages effectively.

### 🎙️ Interview Performance
* **Behavioral Mastery:** Translating past data project challenges into clear "Problem-Action-Result" narratives using the STAR method.
* **Rehearsal Protocols:** Implementing mock interview sessions to refine verbal delivery, technical explanation clarity, and professional presence.
* **Closing Strategy:** Preparing insightful, strategic questions to ask interviewers regarding team dynamics and technical infrastructure.
