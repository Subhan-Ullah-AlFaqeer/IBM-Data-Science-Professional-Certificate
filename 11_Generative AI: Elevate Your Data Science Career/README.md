
# 🤖 IBM Data Science Professional Certificate: Generative AI for Data Science

Welcome to the central repository for **Generative AI: Elevate Your Data Science Career**. This course focuses on the intersection of Large Language Models (LLMs) and the traditional data science lifecycle, providing hands-on skills for automated data engineering, conversational analytics, and ethical AI deployment.

As generative AI becomes mainstream, data professionals must adapt to AI-augmented workflows. This course provides the tactical expertise to leverage GenAI for data generation, augmentation, feature engineering, and automated modeling—essential skills for modern data scientists, analysts, and engineers.

## 🎯 Global Technical Competencies Mastered

### 📊 Data Science Augmentation
* **Automated Feature Engineering:** Leveraging generative models to identify, transform, and normalize features faster than manual scripting.
* **Conversational EDA:** Implementing `LangChain` architectures to interpret statistical distributions through natural language interfaces.
* **Rapid Prototyping:** Utilizing LLMs to bootstrap Scikit-Learn pipelines, reducing "cold-start" time for new analytical projects.

### 🛡️ Ethics & Industry Standards
* **Responsible AI:** Identifying and mitigating data bias, hallucinations, and security risks in automated model outputs.
* **Corporate Governance:** Navigating industry-specific constraints (Finance, Healthcare) regarding data confidentiality and model transparency.
